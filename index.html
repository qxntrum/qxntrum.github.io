QXNTRUM is the recording name of Danylo Heraimovych — a project built to put sound to ideas that didn't fit anywhere else.

It's early, it's hands-on, and everything here is being built one track at a time. No finished catalogue yet, no polished bio — just a producer at the start of something, documenting it as it happens instead of waiting for it to be "ready."
